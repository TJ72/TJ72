## About

> *Learning the cloud by building on it.*

I'm a developer from Taiwan, moving to Sydney in September 2026 for a Master of Information
Technology at UNSW — and open to opportunities there. Most recently I was the sole frontend
engineer on a greenfield settlement platform at [XREX](https://xrex.io), a regulated crypto-fiat
exchange in Taipei. Money software has its own discipline: a rounding error becomes a support
ticket, and a compliance flow is a feature, not a checkbox.

I believe curiosity compounds. I started out centering divs, and three years later I'm running
my blog on infrastructure I designed myself — a Dockerised Next.js app on Cloud Run, 50+
Terraform resources, keyless CI/CD through Workload Identity Federation. Backend and
infrastructure is where most of my own building goes now; it's pinned below.
